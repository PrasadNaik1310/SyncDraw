
Question: What is the difference between cohesion and coupling?

Answer:
 Based on the provided document, here is the difference between cohesion and coupling:

* **Cohesion** is a measure of the functional strength of a module. A cohesive module performs a single task or function (Page 14).
* **Coupling** between two modules is a measure of the degree of interdependence or interaction between the two modules (Page 14). 

Additionally, ideal good software design promotes tight cohesion and low coupling (Page 16).
